Judge what a text does for the user, not how it is phrased:
- Intent: a label or heading must tell what happens or what the area is for; a text that names no outcome fails.
- Duplication: two texts in the focus area that say the same thing, or a text that repeats the visible label beside it.
- Relevance: a text that explains nothing the interface does not already show, or concerns something outside the user's task here.
- Filler: generic, promotional or self-congratulatory text that could sit unchanged on any product.
- Overload: more text than the task needs, competing with the controls for attention.
Quote the text and state which check it fails. Never propose replacement wording.
